Quick guide

Set the OBS to the desired inbound course or outbound radial. Use the CDI and TO/FROM indication to determine the intercept. Choose a heading and watch the aircraft track converge toward the selected course. The magenta line shows the aircraft's present radial, while amber shows the selected course line.

Heading, OBS and wind are modeled in a simplified, flat-earth environment with magnetic north equal to true north. CDI full-scale deflection is ±10°, with a 2°/dot scale. No cone-of-confusion, signal loss or station passage flag logic is simulated.
